# 📶 1.2 Connection Types & Functions
> **Tag:** #it-skills #wireless #connections #module-1

## 📌 Why Wireless Connections Matter in Security
Wireless connections send data through the air using radio waves or light. Since there are no physical cables, anyone nearby with an antenna can intercept the signal. Understanding these connection types is crucial for securing mobile devices, IoT, and office networks.

---

## 🗂️ Wireless Technologies Compared

| Technology | Range | Common Use Cases | Key Security Risks |
|---|---|---|---|
| **NFC (Near Field Communication)** | Very Short (< 4 cm) | Contactless payments (Apple Pay), security keycards, transit passes. | **Skimming**: Unauthorized readers stealing data nearby.<br>**Relay Attacks**: Relaying a signal to a reader far away. |
| **Wi-Fi** | Medium (~50m) | Connecting devices to local networks and the internet. | **Eavesdropping**: Intercepting unencrypted web traffic.<br>**Evil Twin**: Attackers setting up fake Wi-Fi to steal logins. |
| **Bluetooth** | Short (~10m) | Wireless headphones, mice, keyboards, file sharing (AirDrop). | **Bluejacking**: Sending spam messages to your device.<br>**Bluesnarfing**: Unauthorized stealing of contacts/photos. |
| **Infrared (IR)** | Line of Sight | TV remotes, old data transfer. | Generally secure due to physical limitations (cannot pass through walls, requires straight line of sight). |

## 🔒 Deep Dive: Wi-Fi Encryption Protocols
When you connect to Wi-Fi, your connection should be encrypted. Over the years, different protocols have been developed:

1. **WEP (Wired Equivalent Privacy)**: 
   * *Status*: **EXTREMELY BROKEN / OBSOLETE**.
   * *Security Info*: Can be cracked in less than 60 seconds using free tools (like `aircrack-ng`). Never use this!
2. **WPA / WPA2 (Wi-Fi Protected Access)**:
   * *Status*: **Widespread, but vulnerable**.
   * *Security Info*: Vulnerable to handshake capture and offline brute-force cracking.
3. **WPA3**:
   * *Status*: **Modern & Secure**.
   * *Security Info*: Protects against offline dictionary attacks and uses individualized data encryption.

---

## 🛡️ Best Security Practices for Connections
* **For NFC**: Keep payment cards in RFID-blocking wallets. Turn off NFC on your phone when not in use.
* **For Wi-Fi**: Avoid using public, unencrypted Wi-Fi (like coffee shops) without a secure VPN. Use WPA3 or WPA2-Enterprise at home/office.
* **For Bluetooth**: Keep your device hidden (non-discoverable mode) by default. Turn off Bluetooth when you are in crowded public spaces if not needed.
