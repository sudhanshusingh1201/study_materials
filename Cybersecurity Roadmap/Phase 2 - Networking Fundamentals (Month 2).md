# Phase 2: Networking Fundamentals (Month 2)
Networking is the backbone of cybersecurity. You cannot scan or hack what you don't understand.

*   **Core Concepts to Learn:**
    *   **OSI Model & TCP/IP Model:** Deep understanding of all layers.
    *   **IP Addressing:** IPv4, IPv6, Subnetting, MAC addresses.
    *   **Common Protocols & Ports:** HTTP (80), HTTPS (443), SSH (22), DNS (53), DHCP (67/68), FTP (21), SMB (445).
    *   How Routers, Switches, and Firewalls route packets.
*   **Actionable Tasks:**
    *   Learn how to calculate subnets.
    *   Use command line network tools: `ping`, `nslookup`, `traceroute` (`tracert`), `netstat`, `ipconfig` / `ifconfig`.
