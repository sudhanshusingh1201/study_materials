# Phase 6: Professional Certifications & Job Preparation (Month 9-12)
Structure your resume, build a portfolio, and get certified to land a job.

*   **Recommended Entry-Level Certifications:**
    1.  **eJPT (eLearnSecurity Junior Penetration Tester):** 100% practical, hands-on hacking exam. Highly respected for junior roles.
    2.  **CompTIA Security+:** Theory-heavy, globally recognized. Great for security analyst and government/compliance roles.
    3.  **CEH (Certified Ethical Hacker):** Famous name, but mostly multiple-choice questions. Only do this if a job posting specifically asks for it.
*   **Portfolio Building:**
    *   Start a blog (GitHub Pages or Medium) and write detailed walkthroughs of CTF challenges you solve (make sure not to post spoilers for active boxes!).
    *   Upload your custom scripts (e.g., custom scanners, automation scripts) to GitHub.
    *   Build a resume highlighting your lab hours, certifications, and technical skills.

---

## 💡 Top 3 Success Tips
1.  **Do Not Rushing:** Don't skip Phase 1 and 2 to reach Phase 5. If you don't know subnetting or Linux permissions, you will fail at hacking.
2.  **Write Notes:** When you learn a new command or tool, write it down in a personal note-taking tool (like Obsidian or Notion). You will forget syntax easily!
3.  **Join the Community:** Join cybersecurity Discord servers, participate in local CTF competitions, and talk to professionals on LinkedIn. Networking is key.
