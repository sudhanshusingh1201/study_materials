# ⚡ 4. Link Speed, Duplex, & PoE (Power over Ethernet)

### 🏎️ Link Speed & Duplex
* **Link Speed:** Ports ki physical data limit (100 Mbps, 1 Gbps, 10 Gbps).
* **Half-Duplex:** Data dono side se ja sakta hai par ek time par sirf ek hi (jaise Walkie-Talkie).
* **Full-Duplex:** Simultaneous bi-directional data flow (jaise Phone call).
* **Auto-Negotiation:** Do connected devices aapas me negotiate karke best common speed aur duplex speed lock karti hain.

### 🔌 PoE (Power over Ethernet)
PoE ke zariye ek hi single Ethernet cable se **Data aur Electric Power** dono transmit kiye ja sakte hain.
* **PSE (Power Sourcing Equipment):** Jo device power bhejti hai (e.g., PoE Switch).
* **PD (Powered Device):** Jo device power receive karti hai (e.g., IP Camera, VoIP Phone).
* **Standards:**
  - **PoE (802.3af):** Up to 15.4W per port.
  - **PoE+ (802.3at):** Up to 30.0W per port (for heavy PTZ cameras).
  - **PoE++ (802.3bt):** Up to 60W (Type 3) or 100W (Type 4) (for laptops and TVs).
